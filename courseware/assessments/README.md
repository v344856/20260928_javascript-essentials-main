# Assessments

A light, per-module **formative** assessment suite: one short **exit ticket** for each of the course's
twelve modules. These are *for the learner first*, because they surface confusion early so the
instructor can adjust, and they are deliberately fast, low-stakes, and **never graded or ranked**.

## What these are (and aren't)

- **Formative, not summative.** The goal is *signal*, not a score. Nobody passes or fails an exit
  ticket. There is no grade book, no ranking, no "right answer" wall.
- **~5 minutes each.** Three quick recap questions plus two reflection prompts. Anonymous is fine;
  honest muddiness is more useful than a polished answer.
- **Grounded in the actual docs.** Every recap question comes straight from that module's chapters
  (see [`../docs/README.md`](../docs/README.md)), with nothing invented.

### The two tools inside each ticket

1. **The exit ticket**: three recap questions (a mix of multiple choice, short answer, and one
   "explain in your own words"), plus a **Connect It** prompt that ties the module to the learner's
   **prior programming experience in another language** (this course's audience already codes elsewhere).
2. **The Muddiest Point**: a single open question, *"What's the one thing from this module that's
   still fuzzy?"* This is the highest-value line on the page: it tells you exactly what to revisit.

Each ticket ends with a collapsed **Instructor Answer Key**. For the open-ended questions it says
*what to listen for* rather than a single correct answer, and closes with a short note on what the
Muddiest Point / Connect It signals are telling you.

## How to use them (instructor)

- **Run one at the END OF EACH MODULE**, not per day. (Some days span more than one module; some
  modules span parts of two days. Give the ticket when a module wraps.) Budget ~5 minutes.
- **Collect them** (paper, a shared doc, or a quick poll; anonymous is fine).
- **Skim before you build further.** Read the Muddiest Points before the next module; a 2-3 minute
  recap of a common fuzzy spot is the whole point of the exercise.
- **Never grade or rank.** Treat every answer as a gift of information, not a performance. These
  results are for tuning the teaching, not evaluating the learner.
